About this page

Without signal plan: the current corridor. Only I2 has a signal; I1, I3 and I4 are priority junctions. Overall = 7-day average demand; Weekday and Weekend reuse the 7-day turn shares and only change the main-road demand.

Day-type counts: new runs where turn shares, crossing-street volumes and vehicle mix also come from the Mon–Fri or Sat–Sun counts. This is needed because daily counts differ significantly between weekdays and weekends.

With signal plan: the same corridor with signals at I1, I2, I4 (and I3) and tuned cycle and offsets.

Red outlines mark stopped or queued vehicles. One hour is simulated; use the speed buttons to play faster. Each card can be hidden with its – button, or all with “Cards”.
